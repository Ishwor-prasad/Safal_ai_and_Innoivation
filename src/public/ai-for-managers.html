Practical skills you can use the week after.

  • Read faster

    Long reports, policies and manuals become clear one-page briefs.

  • Write less

    Emails, notices, memos and proposals drafted in minutes.

  • Close the loop

    Every meeting ends with decisions, owners and deadlines.

  • Stay organized

    Projects, tasks and deadlines that need less chasing.

  • Understand numbers

    Budgets, sales and KPIs made readable with spreadsheets and AI.

  • Decide with evidence

    Research an issue and arrive at the decision with options ready.

    Turn information into clean updates and presentations.

  • Automate the routine

    Connect your tools so repeat work moves on its own.

Who it is for

Managers and team leads in any industry: banking, insurance, hydropower, NGOs, education, hospitality, retail, project management and small businesses.

No technical background needed. The industry changes; many management problems do not.

The right tool for the job

ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 and Copilot, NotebookLM, spreadsheet tools, research AI, and automation platforms like Make or n8n. We never teach a tool for its own sake.
