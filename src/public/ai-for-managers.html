The seven sessions
Each one builds on the last — by the end, AI is handling the repetitive parts of your week on its own.
01
AI Mindset for Managers
Map your actual week — emails, meetings, reports, decisions — and sort each task into Do, Delegate, Automate, or Eliminate. Score by value, frequency, and risk.
Personal AI Opportunity Map
02
Communicating & Working With AI
Use AI that already lives inside your everyday tools. Build a library of reusable prompts for report writing, meeting summaries, proposals, and research.
Manager Prompt Library
03
Documents, Meetings & Knowledge
Turn scattered documents, policies, and meeting notes into an AI-assisted knowledge process — ask what happened, what changed, and what decisions are needed.
Manager Knowledge Assistant
04
Data & Decision Making
Use AI as an analytical partner — ask what's happening, why, what evidence supports it, and what options exist. The manager remains responsible for the final call.
AI Decision Brief
05
AI Workflow & Automation
Connect AI directly to the tools your team runs on — task board, chat app, document store. One real, repetitive process gets redesigned end to end.
One Working AI Workflow
06
Managing an AI-Powered Team
What should employees hand to AI, how is output verified, which decisions stay human, and what information never goes into a public AI tool?
Team AI Playbook
07
AI Strategy & 30-Day Roadmap
All opportunities scored together and ranked by value, frequency, and risk. Each manager leaves with a week-by-week implementation plan.
30-Day Implementation Plan
