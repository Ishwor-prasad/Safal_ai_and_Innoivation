What you will build

You leave with a workflow, not a folder of prompts. For example:

Meetings→Summary→Action items→Task list
Documents→Analysis→Management brief
Data→Analysis→Management report

Roughly 70% hands-on, 30% concept. Every session produces something you keep and can use the following week.
