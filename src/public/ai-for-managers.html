01

AI Mindset for Managers

Before touching any tool, managers map their actual week — emails, meetings, reports, decisions — and sort each task into Do, Delegate, Automate, or Eliminate. Every task is then scored by value, frequency, and risk, so the day ends with a ranked list, not just an inventory.

Deliverable
Personal AI Opportunity Map — a ranked list of exactly where to start.
02

Communicating & Working With AI

Managers learn to use AI that already lives inside their everyday tools — native scheduling, reporting, and search — before ever opening a prompt window. From there, they build a small library of reusable prompts for the roles they actually need: report writer, meeting summarizer, proposal reviewer, research assistant.

Deliverable
Personal Manager Prompt Library — 10+ reusable prompts built from their own work.
03

Documents, Meetings & Knowledge

Managers turn scattered documents, policies, and meeting notes into an AI-assisted knowledge process — asking what happened, what changed, what risks exist, and what decisions are needed, instead of reading everything themselves cover to cover.

Deliverable
Manager Knowledge Assistant — a repeatable process for turning documents into answers.
04

Data & Decision Making

Working with a real business dataset, managers practice using AI as an analytical partner rather than an oracle — asking what's happening, why, what evidence supports it, and what options exist. The manager remains responsible for the final call; AI's job is to shorten the distance to a good decision.

Deliverable
AI Decision Brief — a one-page document: problem, evidence, options, recommendation.
05

AI Workflow & Automation

Managers connect AI directly to the tools their team already runs on — a task board, a chat app, a document store — instead of working around a chatbot in a separate tab. One real, repetitive process, chosen back on Day 1, gets redesigned end to end and tested live.

Deliverable
One working AI-powered workflow, built on their own backlog or inbox.
06

Managing an AI-Powered Team

Managers address the questions that come after adoption: what should employees hand to AI, how is output verified, which decisions must stay human, and what information should never go into a public AI tool. This is where AI stops being a personal habit and starts being a team standard.

Deliverable
Team AI Playbook — a one-page policy covering approved uses, review requirements, and data rules.
07

AI Strategy & 30-Day Roadmap

All identified opportunities are scored together and ranked by value, frequency, and risk. Each manager leaves with a week-by-week plan: select a problem, build and test, run it against real work, then measure and standardize what works.

Deliverable
A signed-off 30-Day AI Implementation Roadmap.

Who This Is For

Participants

Managers and team leads, operations and admin staff, and anyone responsible for a team's output. No coding, and no technical background required.

What You Bring

One real, recurring problem from your own work — a report you dread, a backlog nobody triages, an inbox that eats your mornings, or a decision that keeps stalling. The course is built around solving it, not around a hypothetical case study.

Format

Roughly 70% hands-on, 30% concept. Every session produces something you keep and can use the following week.

What You Leave With

  • Personal AI Opportunity Map
  • Manager Prompt Library
  • Manager Knowledge Assistant
  • AI Decision Brief
  • One working AI-powered workflow
  • Team AI Playbook
  • 30-Day AI Implementation Roadmap
Not a certificate. A working system.
